Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

BizMate ("we", "our", or "the app") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the BizMate mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

BizMate is designed with a strict privacy-first, offline-first architecture:

  • All business data—including transactions, customer contacts, sales, invoices, and camera rental orders—is stored locally on your device in secure on-device databases.
  • We do not operate central application servers that collect, store, or profile your personal or business records.

2. Google Drive API Usage & Scopes

BizMate offers an optional cloud backup and restore feature powered by the official Google Drive API. When you choose to link your Google account:

  • Scope Requested: We request the minimum restricted scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.
  • Limited Access: This scope only gives BizMate access to create, read, and update files created specifically by BizMate (inside a dedicated folder named "BizMate Backups"). BizMate cannot see, read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Data Handling: Your backup archives are uploaded directly from your device to your personal Google Drive account. We never intercept, redirect, or inspect the contents of your backup files.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

BizMate adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing, or credit-worthiness purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

4. Third-Party Services

BizMate does not embed third-party advertising networks or tracking software. Google authentication is handled directly via official Google SDKs (Google Sign-In for Android/iOS).

5. Data Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • Local Data: You can wipe all local records at any time directly through the app settings or by clearing app storage in device settings.
  • Cloud Backups: You can delete any backup file at any time by opening your Google Drive and removing the "BizMate Backups" folder.
